Saved Index — user journey
v2 · popup onboarding + full-page library
The extension does its reading in a small popup and its work on the page itself. Once a key is connected, the saved-posts grid is replaced in place: same page, same scroll, now with summaries, categories and dates. Nothing is squeezed into a 400px column.
01
Connect a provider
Popup. Pick one of four providers, paste a key, test it. Two clicks and a paste.
02
Choose a model
Models are listed for the provider you picked, with a cost per 1,000 posts.
03
Index in the background
One strip on the page counts up. Pause, close the tab, resume later.
04
Scan and filter
The page itself becomes the library: 20 posts per screen, categories down the left.
05
Open one post
Summary beside the video, and a scrubber thin enough to forget.
